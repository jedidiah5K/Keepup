## Dropped while checking

- **Tickmates**: an app called Tickmate (habit journal) is on the App Store and F-Droid.
- **Duostreak**: too close to Duolingo, which is famous for streaks.
- **Squadstreak / Streaksquad**: .com taken; also an app called "Squad Habits" exists (small group habit tracking), so it would be easy to mix up.
- Short words like Ember, Rally, Kiln, Stoke, Upkeep, Tally: all .app and .com taken.

## A note on "streak"

"Streaks" is the name of a well-known iPhone habit app. Using "streak" inside a longer
made-up word (Crewstreak, Kinstreak) is normal and low risk, but don't call the app just
"Streak" or "Streaks".

## My pick

**Crewstreak** or **Kinstreak**. Both are free on .com and .app, have no app clash that I
could find, and explain the squad idea instantly.
