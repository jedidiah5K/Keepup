## Found by the audit, not fixed (bigger changes, your call)
- **Reminder job can be swamped:** one account with hundreds of habits at the same reminder time could push other people's reminders past the 1,000-row limit, and those would be marked sent but never arrive. The fix is a habit cap per account (for example 100) plus paging in the reminder function. This changes a rule ("unlimited habits"), so it needs your OK.
- **No per-account caps** on habits, breaks or push tokens. Bulk junk could fill the free 500 MB database that Petal shares. Same fix as above, plus the sign-up bot check.
- **Breaks can start yesterday,** which wipes yesterday's miss, and breaks can be chained back to back. This is a game rule, so it's your call.
- **Small race conditions** on the try limits (two requests at the exact same moment). Low risk.
- **A bad timezone value** might break the reminder job. Needs checking on the real database.
- **At 200% text,** the welcome screen's buttons cover the headline until you scroll. The other screens haven't been checked at big text yet.
