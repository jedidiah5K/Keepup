Privacy

Beta version, last updated 23 October 2026. A full policy will be published before KeepUp launches in the app stores.

The short version

KeepUp keeps your habits on your phone. If you make an account, they're also stored in our database so you can use them on other devices. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising tools.

What we store

If you make an account: your email, a scrambled (hashed) password, your name, your settings, your habits and check-ins. If you join a squad, your squadmates see your name and the habits you choose to share, nothing else.

Where

Our database is hosted by Supabase. The web app is hosted by Vercel.

Your control

You can export everything from Me → Your data, at any time. To delete your account and all its data, go to Me → Your data → Delete account. It is removed from our servers straight away.

Age

KeepUp is for people 13 and older.

Contact

Message the person who invited you to the beta. A support email will be listed here before launch.
